Per-Ax

A League of Legends companion for the LAN server: guidance in champ select, on the loading screen and in game, built to stay inside Riot's developer policies.

What it does

  • Champ select: pick and ban suggestions for your role, rune and summoner import, and matchup notes. Clicking a suggestion only hovers it; picking, banning and accepting are always the player's own clicks.
  • Loading screen: a scout of the ten players from public ranked history (rank, recent form, champion record).
  • In game: objective timers, tempo and safety guidance, and item suggestions, read from the game's local Live Client Data API.
  • Between games: a profile that grades your own games and points at the habit costing you the most LP.

How it uses Riot's data

  • Read-only: it never sends keyboard or mouse input to the game or the client, and never automates champ-select decisions.
  • Enemy information is limited to what the game already showed the player (kill feed, objective announcements, scoreboard). Nothing is inferred from fog of war, and players the client anonymizes are never looked up.
  • Riot API (account-v1, summoner-v4, league-v4, champion-mastery-v4, match-v5) with Riot's rate-limit headers respected. Ranked games are aggregated locally into item statistics without storing the names or PUUIDs of the players in them (only the ladder players a collection run starts from are kept, by PUUID, so it can resume).
